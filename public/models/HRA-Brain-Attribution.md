# Brain, Female — HRA reference model

- Source: [NIH 3D entry 3DPX-020959, version 2](https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3DPX-020959?version=2)
- Model publisher: Human Reference Atlas (HRA)
- HRA object: [Brain-female v1.3](https://purl.humanatlas.io/ref-organ/brain-female/v1.3)
- Downloaded asset: `hra-brain-female-v1.3.glb` (11,977,884 bytes)

- Source basis: Allen Human Reference Atlas — 3D, 2020 (Ding et al., 2016), as described by NIH 3D.

The model's named cortical meshes are retained as non-rendering depth and region-picking surfaces. Neural points and short local paths are sampled from its named gyrus, lobule, precuneus, cuneus, and pole meshes. The scene recenters and uniformly scales the model, then renders a merged solid cortical surface with theme-aware vertex colors, with the sampled points and paths overlaid as a neural-network visualization. The five interactive territories are a conceptual visual metaphor and do not represent scientifically assigned brain functions.
